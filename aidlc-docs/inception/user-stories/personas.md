# Student Analytics Portfolio — Personas

These three personas are archetypes for the approved revamp; they do not represent invented people or additional product audiences.

## P-01 — Admissions or Internship Reviewer

- Role: university/admissions reviewer or recruiter evaluating a high-school student's readiness and interests.
- Goals: identify the student's background quickly; assess real analytics experience, project methods, outcomes, and learning; obtain the student's resume.
- Context: a limited review window, on desktop or mobile.
- Needs: a clear student identity, accurate academic status and dates, understandable evidence, and no unsupported qualifications or fake artifacts.
- Friction: stale owner information, vague project claims, broken links, and unreadable layouts reduce trust.
- Relevant stories: SA-US-01, SA-US-02, SA-US-03, SA-US-04, SA-US-05, SA-US-06, SA-US-07.

## P-02 — Mentor or Peer

- Role: teacher, mentor, fellow student, or potential collaborator exploring the student's interests.
- Goals: understand how biology, mathematics, analytics, and sustainability connect; review projects and community work; recognize which contact details are available.
- Context: reading across mobile/desktop, with personal presentation and color preferences.
- Needs: approachable explanations, clear sections, accessible controls, and honest distinction between available evidence and placeholders.
- Friction: overstatement of experience, fictitious data graphics, distracting effects, or contact actions that appear usable but do not reach the student.
- Relevant stories: SA-US-01, SA-US-02, SA-US-03, SA-US-04, SA-US-05, SA-US-07.

## P-03 — Student Portfolio Owner

- Role: the student maintaining this portfolio's shared content and contact configuration.
- Goals: present supported accomplishments consistently in both styles; replace placeholder details later; retain the existing static delivery and display experience.
- Context: updating content through the existing repository workflow; this revamp does not introduce a CMS or new deployment journey.
- Needs: shared student data, clear placeholder status, correct resume access, and removed obsolete owner assets/content.
- Friction: duplicated facts, stale identity strings, confusing placeholder destinations, and build failures after asset cleanup.
- Relevant stories: SA-US-01, SA-US-05, SA-US-06, SA-US-07, SA-US-08.
